Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I add a new vehicle?
A: Tap the "+" button on the main screen, then select "Add Vehicle." Enter your vehicle information including make, model, year, and VIN (optional).
Q: How do I set up maintenance reminders?
A: Go to your vehicle details, tap "Maintenance," then "Add Reminder." Choose the service type and set either a date-based or mileage-based reminder.
Q: Can I export my maintenance history?
A: Yes! Go to Settings > Data > Export Data. You can export your maintenance history as a PDF or CSV file.
Q: How do I sync my data across devices?
A: Create an account in Settings > Account. Once signed in, your data will automatically sync across all devices where you're logged in.
Q: Is my data secure?
A: Yes! All data is encrypted both in transit and at rest. We use industry-standard security practices to protect your information. See our Privacy Policy for more details.
Q: How do I delete my account?
A: Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account. Please note that this action is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data will be permanently deleted.
Q: The app is crashing. What should I do?
A: First, try restarting the app. If the issue persists, try updating to the latest version. If you're still experiencing issues, please contact our support team with details about when the crash occurs.
Q: How do I restore my data after getting a new phone?
A: If you had an account and cloud sync enabled, simply download the app on your new device and sign in. Your data will automatically sync. Without cloud sync, data cannot be transferred between devices.
